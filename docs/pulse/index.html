Reading the tape ▮ between quarters

Higher-frequency leading indicators that take the pulse of financial markets between quarterly heat-map updates. These are raw market series in their own units — not heat quantiles — drawn from public sources and refreshed by the weekly pipeline. Proxies are labeled as proxies; indicators without a confirmed public source are marked honestly rather than charted.

Market Pulse is the higher-frequency companion to the FRB Heat Index replication — a public-data rebuild of the Federal Reserve's heat map of U.S. financial-system vulnerabilities. The pulse is organized the way a large investment bank looks at the world: deal flow, trading desks, equities, asset management, firm-wide funding — plus funding plumbing, positioning, filings, sentiment, and cross-market signals.
