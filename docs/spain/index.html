Spain: where the financial system stands

An experimental second country section: the U.S. heat index construction applied to Spain, by analogy, component by component — including one component the U.S. index does not have (the sovereign–bank nexus) and several it cannot have filled the same way. This page is an experiment: it is not linked from the site index or the navigation and is reachable only by direct URL. Every indicator is percentile-transformed against its own Spanish history, so 0.9 means "higher than 90% of Spain's own past", not a comparison with the United States. Data through 2026Q2; headline 0.339, against a sample peak of 0.759 in 2007Q3. 17 indicators live, 9 source-pending, 12 of 16 components in the headline. Construction, the analogy and its limits: methodology.

Mean of the component percentiles below. Spain's sample contains a full systemic crisis (2010–13), so the scale is calibrated by a real event.

Maturity transformation — indicators

Deposit Institutions' Loans to Non-Bank Residents / Deposits of Other Resident Sectors (Banco de España)

Latest raw value: 72.10 (2026Q2) · percentile 0.005

Source: Banco de España, Boletín Estadístico, tables 8.33 and 8.34 (deposit institutions' balance sheet)
Series: BE_8_33.8, BE_8_34.7
Definition: 100 x (deposit institutions' loans and credits to non-bank residents in Spain — general government and other resident sectors, BE_8_33.8) / (deposits of other resident sectors at deposit institutions, BE_8_34.7), percent, quarter-end month. The funding gap that defined Spanish bank balance sheets before 2012: a ratio well above 100 means lending funded by wholesale markets and the Eurosystem rather than by deposits. The Banco de España's own stability-report variant, which adds securitised loans back to credit and strips repos from deposits, prints higher; this is the balance-sheet-tables variant, computed from two published aggregates with no adjustment.
Frequency: Monthly (quarter-end) · Observations: 1999Q1–2026Q2 (n=110) · Chart: percentile against own history, 0–1.

Net saving — indicators

Household Net Financial Saving, Percent of GDP (Banco de España, financial accounts)

Latest raw value: 2.91 (2026Q2) · percentile 0.159

Source: Banco de España, Cuentas Financieras de la Economía Española (Boletín Estadístico, table 2.7)
Series: BE_2_7.7
Definition: Households' and NPISH net financial transactions (net acquisition of financial assets minus net incurrence of liabilities) as a percent of GDP, as published by the Banco de España (BE_2_7.7, 'ratio sobre el PIB'). Labelled a proxy: it is the financial-accounts counterpart of the U.S. NIPA net saving rate, not the national-accounts saving rate itself; oriented so that lower (more negative) saving reads as more vulnerable. Frequency: Quarterly · Observations: 1999Q1–2026Q2 (n=110) · Chart: percentile against own history, 0–1.
