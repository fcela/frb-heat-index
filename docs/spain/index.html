Spain: where the financial system stands

An experimental second country section: the U.S. heat index construction applied to Spain, by analogy, component by component — including one component the U.S. index does not have (the sovereign–bank nexus) and several it cannot have filled the same way. This page is an experiment: it is not linked from the site index or the navigation and is reachable only by direct URL. Every indicator is percentile-transformed against its own Spanish history, so 0.9 means "higher than 90% of Spain's own past", not a comparison with the United States. Data through 2026Q2; headline 0.415, against a sample peak of 0.744 in 2007Q4. 23 indicators live, 8 source-pending, 13 of 16 components in the headline. Construction, the analogy and its limits: methodology.

Mean of the component percentiles below. Spain's sample contains a full systemic crisis (2010–13), so the scale is calibrated by a real event.
