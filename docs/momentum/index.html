Pulse of the Market · leading indicators & rotation

What is changing, ▮ not just where things stand

The heat map and Early Warning say where things stand. This section is about movement: leading indicators first, inflection points as dated, computable events, and rotation underneath. Every fingerprint state below is a stated definition evaluated on the section's own archived daily series — the board never reads a value the build did not store. Prices are unadjusted closes from Yahoo Finance; macro and volatility series come from FRED; the excess bond premium from the Federal Reserve's FEDS Notes file. All sources are free. Refreshed weekly on Mondays; the archived series are daily, so the dated inflections are exact.

The fingerprint board

Named market states, each a definition, not a judgement. The measured values behind every state are shown, so any state can be checked against its own bar.

Leading indicators

The series that historically move before the coincident picture does — credit spreads, financial conditions and their subindexes, the volatility complex, the curve, claims, sentiment. Trailing year, own units, full provenance on every panel.

Inflection log

Turning points as dated events. Each signal has a computable definition; the log gives the last date it fired, not a narrative about what it means.

Rotation

Relative-strength quadrants computed from closes — trend is the 63-session return spread against SPY, momentum the 21-session spread — stated as a table with the two measured numbers, and the named patterns the quadrants and the fingerprint states jointly define.

Sector momentum grid

The evidence layer: every panel on the same rebased scale (100 = 63 sessions ago), with 5/21/63-session returns, distance from the 52-week high, and 21-session realized volatility. No panel is cherry-picked; the grid is the universe the breadth and rotation numbers are computed from.

Cross-asset movers & the vol regime

The cross-asset statement of the same windows — levels move in percentage points (shown in basis points), prices in percent — and the volatility complex: spot VIX against 3-month VXV, with their spread, whose sign is the inversion fingerprint.

Pulse of the Market is the momentum companion to the FRB Heat Index replication and Early Warning. For funding, liquidity, and financial conditions — whether the system is straining — see Early Warning. Every state definition, threshold, and data route is stated in the methodology. Data: ../data/momentum.json, refreshed weekly (Mondays) by the momentum.yml pipeline from free public sources only; per-series daily CSVs under data/momentum/. Prices are unadjusted closes, the repository's standing convention; windows are counted in sessions, so a state dated "since" a day names the session the definition first held.
