Methodology

Target. OFR Hedge Fund Monitor, qualifying hedge funds: gross assets (FPF-ALLQHF_GAV_SUM) over net assets (FPF-ALLQHF_NAV_SUM), quarterly from 2013Q1 (54 observations). The same series appears in the quarterly panel as “Hedge Fund Leverage (Gross assets to net assets, qualifying hedge funds)”.

Publication rule. Quarter Q is treated as published at the end of week 10 of Q+1, matching the OFR's observed cadence (the 2026Q2 print was live by mid-September 2026). The most recent published value anchors every estimate.

Model. A ridge regression (λ = 100) with a mean-drift intercept predicts the change from the last published value. A levels regression is not used: the series drifts (1.8–1.9 in 2013–17, 2.52 in 2026Q2), and a levels fit shrinks toward the historical mean and fails; unregularized OLS is unusable at this sample size. Features, computed quarter-to-date at each Friday vintage from FRED's keyless graph CSVs: S&P 500 cumulative log return, mean VIX, mean Baa–Treasury spread (BAA minus DGS10), change in the 10-year yield, change in the 2-year yield, change in the broad dollar index (log), weeks since the last publication, and the fraction of the quarter elapsed. Training rows are built the same ragged way from each published quarter in turn.

Backtest. Pseudo-real-time over Friday vintages from January 2019 to September 2026, using final published values as targets (the OFR does not publish a vintage history; this flatters all models, benchmarks included, equally). In-progress quarter: RMSE 0.0758 for the ridge against 0.0845 for carry-forward. Just-ended quarter, estimated about ten weeks before it posts: 0.0509 against 0.0557. The band shown next to each estimate is the matching backtest RMSE.

Cross-check. The SCOOS dealer survey (FRED EXHE2C1Q8NP, net percent reporting increased use of leverage) posts inside the quarter it covers, so it is displayed as an observed timely cross-check next to the nowcast — it is never modeled. If a quarter's value has not posted by mid-quarter it is labeled “pending”.

Limits. Fifty-four quarterly observations and one publication-lag assumption. The backtest edge over carry-forward is about 10% at every horizon but is not precisely estimated, and moves driven by positioning rather than market prices (the 2020Q1 spike to 2.18, then 1.99) are only partly anticipated.

Grading. Every weekly run archives its estimates in data/hf_nowcast_vintages.csv. When the OFR print for a quarter lands (the 2026Q3 print is expected in December 2026), src/hf_grade.py scores every archived vintage for that quarter against the actual — RMSE of the nowcast against RMSE of carry-forward, mean error, and the per-vintage table — and the results will be reported here.
