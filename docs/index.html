FRB Heat Index

A replication of the Federal Reserve's heat map of financial-system vulnerabilities ("Mapping Heat in the U.S. Financial System", FEDS Notes 2015 and the published journal version), rebuilt indicator-by-indicator from public data. Every cell is a quantile in its own history: blue is subdued, red is acute.
